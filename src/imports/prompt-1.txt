====================================================
PRODUCT CONTEXT
====================================================

CRYPTAVISTA is a comprehensive Enterprise Cryptographic Discovery & Analysis Tool designed to help organizations prepare for the transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography.

The application must support the complete ECDAT workflow:

DISCOVER
Find and catalogue cryptographic artefacts across enterprise applications and infrastructure.

VERIFY
Use runtime evidence to distinguish cryptography discovered statically from cryptographic operations actually observed during monitored application execution.

ASSESS
Classify cryptographic artefacts and assess quantum risk using cryptographic context, data lifetime, business criticality and Mosca-based risk analysis.

PRIORITIZE
Compare applications and cryptographic assets to determine which systems require attention first based on combined risk, urgency, impact and migration considerations.

RECOMMEND
Provide suitable PQC or Hybrid cryptography directions based on the application's risk profile, latency, cost and operational context.

The application must clearly support multiple analyzed applications or scan targets.

Each application may have different:

- Cryptographic assets
- Algorithms
- Versions
- Modes
- Protocols
- Certificates
- Cryptographic libraries
- Data protection lifetime
- Business criticality
- Runtime evidence
- Quantum risk
- Estimated migration duration
- Dependency impact
- Priority
- PQC or Hybrid recommendation

This difference must be visually obvious.

Do not design a generic dashboard where every application appears to have identical results.
