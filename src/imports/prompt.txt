Design a complete, polished, enterprise-grade web application prototype named:

CRYPTAVISTA

Supporting product description:
Enterprise Cryptographic Discovery & Analysis Tool (ECDAT)

Tagline:
Discover → Verify → Assess → Prioritize

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:
ECDAT is the Smart India Hackathon problem statement category:
Enterprise Cryptographic Discovery & Analysis Tool.
CRYPTAVISTA is the actual product name designed for this solution.

The application helps organizations discover cryptographic assets across enterprise applications, verify which cryptographic operations are actually observed during runtime, assess future quantum risk, analyze dependency impact and blast radius, and prioritize systems for PQC or hybrid migration planning.

DESIGN STYLE:
Create a professional cybersecurity and enterprise analytics product.
The design must feel like a real enterprise security platform, not a student project, gaming interface, crypto trading dashboard, or AI-generated futuristic UI.

Use a LIGHT THEME.

Do NOT make the interface dark.
Do NOT use neon colors.
Do NOT use excessive gradients.
Do NOT use rainbow colors.
Do NOT make it overly colorful or decorative.
Avoid a feminine, playful, rounded social-media style.

The visual style should be:
- Elegant
- Clean
- Premium
- Professional
- Enterprise-oriented
- Technically credible
- Calm and easy to understand
- Modern without looking futuristic or artificial

COLOR SYSTEM:
Primary: deep enterprise blue
Secondary: muted navy / slate blue
Accent positive: subtle teal or green
High risk: controlled red
Medium risk: amber/orange
Low risk: muted blue/green
Background: very light gray or off-white
Cards: white
Borders: subtle light gray
Text: dark charcoal

Use colors only to communicate meaning and risk.
Maintain strong visual hierarchy and plenty of whitespace.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Use Inter, IBM Plex Sans, or a similar professional enterprise sans-serif font.
Large bold headings.
Clear medium-weight section headings.
Comfortable readable body text.
Avoid oversized text and excessive bold formatting.

LAYOUT:
Desktop enterprise dashboard, approximately 1440px wide.
Persistent left sidebar navigation.
Top header with:
- Breadcrumb or page title
- Global search
- Notifications
- User profile
- "New Analysis" button

The dashboard should feel like one connected analysis workflow.

SIDEBAR NAVIGATION:

1. Overview
2. Projects
3. Discovery
4. Runtime Evidence
5. Crypto Profile
6. Quantum Risk Timeline
7. Dependency & Blast Radius
8. Prioritization
9. Recommendations
10. Reports
11. Settings

At the bottom show the currently selected project and its analysis status.
